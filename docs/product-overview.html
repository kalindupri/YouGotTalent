For talent

Build a profile, get discovered

Any creative category — not just modeling. Acting, singing, dancing, painting, script writing, photography, music, choreography, comedy, voice over, direction, design, and more, each with its own relevant profile fields.

Portfolio & audition media

Photos, videos, audio, or documents — with video/audio automatically compressed on upload to keep storage costs down. Plus a dedicated intro video and a cover photo.

/talents/[id]

Category-specific attributes

An actor's height/build/eye colour has no equivalent for a script writer — each category gets its own structured detail fields and a tailored "special skills" question.

Credits & experience history

A résumé of past projects (film, TV, theatre, commercial, voice, music, event, online) that shows up right on the public profile.

Verification badge

Talent can request verification; admins review the queue and approve or reject — a visible trust signal on search results and profiles.

Premium tier

Free tier has sensible limits (3 portfolio items, 1 audition video); Premium unlocks unlimited items, 5 videos, boosted search placement, and verification priority.

Availability & bookings

Talent sets weekly availability windows; recruiters book engagements directly against open slots.

For recruiters & productions

Find exactly who you need

Individuals and agencies alike post talent hunts, search the full talent pool, and run their hiring pipeline without leaving the platform.

Advanced talent search

Filter by category, city, keyword, experience range, and verified-only — ranked by relevance, not just recency.

/talents

Saved searches with alerts

Save a filter set once; get notified by email when new talent matching it joins.

Shortlisting

Bookmark promising talent to a private saved list, separate from any specific casting call.

Direct invitations

Invite a specific talent to apply or audition for a role, rather than waiting for them to find the listing.

Applicant tracking board

Every application to a posting in one board — shortlist, reject, or accept, per role.

/dashboard/casting-calls/[id]

Recruiter analytics

Views, applications, and response rate per posting — a read on what's actually working.

The core loop

Casting calls & applications

A single posting can carry several distinct roles at once — a feature film casting call might need three different characters, each tracked and applied to separately.

Multi-role postings

Each role has its own criteria and compensation; applicants apply to a specific role, not the posting as a whole.

Rich posting detail

Audition brief (what to perform), shoot dates & locations, compensation, application deadline, and free-text tags for discovery.

Apply with upload

Submit an application with an optional file upload or external submission link, tracked through pending → shortlisted → accepted/rejected.

Featured & view-tracked

Premium recruiters get automatic "Featured" placement; every posting tracks its own view count.

Once you're in touch

Messaging & bookings

The relationship doesn't end at "applied" — talent and recruiters can talk, schedule, and rate each other afterward.

Direct messaging

1:1 conversations between a talent and a recruiter, optionally tied to a specific casting call, with read receipts and an unread badge in the header.

/messages

Scheduled bookings

Book an engagement against a talent's open availability, with a response/cancel flow and an agreement-status field.

Post-booking reviews

Either side can rate and comment after a booking completes — a reputation layer independent of the casting-call system.

Follows

Talent can follow a recruiter or agency to keep an eye on their future postings.

Newest addition — growth play

Community: rate, critique, discuss

Sri Lanka doesn't have a strong home-grown film/TV/song rating & critique site. Built to fill that gap and pull in visitors who aren't here to hire or be hired yet — open to browse, login required to contribute.

Rate & critique

Film / TV / song catalog

Anyone with an account (talent or recruiter) can add a title and rate it 1–5 stars with an optional written critique.

/community/titles
Rate & critique

IMDb-style score display

Poster grid, top-rated/newest/most-reviewed sorting, and a detail page with a big aggregate score plus a full 5-to-1-star rating breakdown.

Discussions

Industry discussion board

Threaded discussions tagged Films, TV, Music, Industry News, or General — optionally linked to a specific catalog title.

/community/discussions
Discussions

Author attribution

Every post shows the author's real talent or recruiter identity and role — no anonymous drive-by content.

Keeping the platform clean

Trust & safety

One moderation system, reused everywhere — a profile, a posting, a message, a title, a critique, a discussion thread, or a reply can all be reported through the same flow.

User-submitted reports

A report button on reportable content, plus a separate bug-report path for site issues — both land in one admin queue.

Verification queues

Separate approve/reject queues for talent and recruiter verification requests.

One-click content removal

Admins can delete the exact reported item — a title, a critique, a thread, a reply — directly from the report, no separate hunt needed.

Discord alerts

New reports and unhandled application errors post straight to a Discord channel, so nothing waits for someone to check a dashboard.

How the business makes money

Billing & subscriptions

A free tier that's genuinely usable, and a premium tier priced to convert early — with the pricing machinery built to change over time without ever surprising an existing subscriber.

Founding-member pricing

Currently LKR 490/mo for talent, LKR 1,500/mo for recruiters (10 months charged annually — "2 months free").

/pricing

Admin-configurable, versioned

Admins change the current price from the dashboard, with a full history of every change and who made it.

/admin/pricing

Cancel anytime, no surprises

Access continues through the period already paid for; a one-time 10%-off-for-3-months offer is shown before a cancellation is finalized, and the reason is recorded.

Failed-payment handling

A grace period with a reminder email before quietly downgrading to free — no account gets cut off mid-cycle without warning.

Pluggable payment gateway

Mock for local testing, PayHere for Sri Lankan LKR payments today, Stripe wired in and ready for international expansion.

Full payment ledger

Every charge and failure is recorded per subscriber — visible to that subscriber as billing history, and to admins in the subscriptions view.

The one guarantee we built in on purpose: a subscriber's price is locked in at the moment they sign up and never changes for them, even if we raise prices later for new signups. Verified by test and by hand before this was ever shipped.

Running the platform

A real admin console

Worth raising in the room

Roadmap & open questions

Not gaps hiding from anyone — the honest list of what's next, useful for a brainstorm or an investor conversation about where funding or headcount would go first.

Blocked

Automatic deploys

The CI/CD pipeline is built but blocked on a one-time GitHub permissions grant — deploys are manual until then.

Gap

Stripe pricing isn't versioned

The Sri Lanka (PayHere) pricing path has full grandfathering; the international Stripe path doesn't yet — a real decision point before expanding beyond Sri Lanka.

Compliance

Guardian consent for minors

Under-18 talent submitting auditions will need a guardian-consent flow before this can be publicly marketed — flagged since the first PDPA review.
